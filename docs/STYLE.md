# STYLE.md — Art Direction Bible

**Concept:** "A friendly corner of the future" — a cozy low-poly floating island
drifting through a warm pastel sky. Ghibli warmth × SpaceX optimism × Monument
Valley cuteness. Delightful, lighthearted, optimistic. Never dark, hacker-y,
or corporate.

**Master reference:** `docs/concept-keyart.jpg` (locked 2026-08-20).
All generated assets must derive from this image (use as edit/reference input)
or from the palette below. No fresh unanchored prompts.

## Palette (night) — cozy periwinkle, never black

sky `#3E4470 → #5A5F92`, grass `#5E8A74`, windows glow brighter (`#FFD98A`,
emissive up), moonlight `#9BA7E8`, stars/fireflies appear.

## Shape & material language

- Low-poly primitives, rounded/chunky silhouettes, no sharp thin edges
- Matte materials (roughness ≈ 0.9), soft shadows, one warm sun
- Proportions: big roofs, small doors, chubby animals

## Don't list

- No photorealism, neon, glassmorphism, purple gradients, pure black
- No sharp/hacker aesthetics, no glitch effects
- No asset that doesn't share this palette or lighting mood
